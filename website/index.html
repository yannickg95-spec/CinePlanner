How it works
📄

Import your
script.

Drop in a screenplay as PDF and CinePlanner detects your scenes automatically from the slug lines. Keep the script beside your shot list and work scene by scene — INT/EXT, day/night, characters and all.

  • Automatic scene detection from INT./EXT. slug lines
  • Multiple script versions side by side, without overwriting
  • Mark script coverage — which shots cover which lines
🎬

Build shot lists that speak the language of the set.

Capture size, movement, lens, grip and camera per shot — prime or zoom, with focal length or range. Camera metadata reads itself from your reference photos' EXIF.

  • Numbering your way — 1.2, 1A or 001
  • Custom info fields for anything your production tracks
  • Built-in film-stock calculator (35 / 16 / 65mm)
  • Reference photos and videos per shot
🗺️

Block it with
real optics.

Draw top-down blocking maps in Shot Designer style — characters and cameras on a canvas. Each camera shows a true field-of-view wedge based on sensor format (Super 16 / Super 35 / Large Format) and the lens you chose. Not symbolic arrows — real coverage.

  • Drop in your own floor plan as an underlay
  • Labels, markers and walk paths for characters
🌅

A schedule built for the shoot day.

Spread scenes across shoot days by drag-and-drop, pick which shots you take each day and in what order. CinePlanner shows sun and golden-hour times per day from the location and date — and warns you when a shot is double-booked.

  • Sunrise, golden hour AM/PM and sunset per shoot day
  • Double-booking detection across days
On set
📱

Your shoot day,
on your lock screen.

Switch to On-Set mode: a full-screen shoot day you check off shot by shot, with the current setup blown up large — size, movement, lens, grip, notes and a framing reference. Tap REF and MAP to pull up the shot's references and scene plan.

  • Drag to re-order shots right there on the floor
  • Live Activity on iPhone lock screen & Dynamic Island
  • Check off the running and next setup without opening the app
  • Progress at a glance — shots done, scenes wrapped

Features

Everything from prep
to the shoot day.

One continuous chain — script, shot list, blocking, schedule, set — instead of a spreadsheet, a drawing tool, some PDFs and a group chat.

🎬

Shot lists

Size, movement, lens, grip and camera per shot. Numbering as 1.2, 1A or 001. Custom fields and a film-stock calculator built in.

🗺️

Blocking with real optics

Top-down maps where each camera shows a true field-of-view wedge from sensor format and lens — not symbolic arrows.

🌅

Schedule with golden hour

Spread scenes across shoot days with sun and golden-hour times per day, plus double-booking detection.

📱

On-Set mode & Live Activity

A full-screen, check-it-off shoot day with the current setup large — and a Live Activity on your iPhone lock screen.

📷

Camera metadata, automatically

EXIF from your reference photos fills in camera family, format, focal length and framelines for you.

📤

Share without the fuss

PDF shot lists per day or scene, a self-contained web export with media, or publish a shareable online page.

🖥️

Across your Apple devices

Mac is the planning office, iPad refines and presents, iPhone is the set companion. One universal app, same projects everywhere.

🔒

Reliable on the floor

iCloud sync, offline-first, automatic local backups and recovery, and full undo/redo on every edit.

CineStager bridge

Import AR setups and top-down maps from the companion app CineStager through a shared iCloud folder, read-only.
