Script-to-shot breakdown
📄

Read your script beside your shot list.

Import your screenplay PDF and view it directly alongside your shot list. Scenes are organized automatically with location headers, INT/EXT tags, DAY/NIGHT indicators and shot counters — so you break the page down without ever leaving it.

  • Side-by-side screenplay reader and shot list
  • Automatic scene breakdown with INT/EXT and DAY/NIGHT
  • Per-scene shot counters keep coverage at a glance
🎬

Log every shot, the way you shoot.

Specify shot size (Close Up, Wide…), shot type (Single, OTS, Top Shot), focal lengths and the exact camera gear — and choose the grip that puts it there.

  • Grip setups: Tripod, Handheld, Dolly, Steadicam, Gimbal, Crane
  • Attach lookbook, previz or location reference frames
  • Log bodies, lens sets, focal lengths, aspect ratios and formats
  • Camera metadata reads itself from your reference photos' EXIF
Signature feature
🖍️

See what covers
every line.

Highlight passages of dialogue or action in the built-in screenplay reader, and CinePlanner draws thin vertical coverage lines in the margin — numbered and color-coded to each shot. It's an instant visual map of which camera setups cover which lines.

  • Tap-to-select word picker to mark the script
  • Color-coded, numbered coverage lines per shot
  • Spot uncovered lines before you step on set
🗺️

Block it on an
overhead map.

Place camera icons, actor markers and floor-plan maps to plan scenes in 2D, and draw actor and camera moves across the map to communicate complex blocking to your crew. Drop a real satellite map under any scene, pan to your exact location and set which way is North.

  • Movement paths for actors and cameras
  • Satellite locations with a North reference
  • Sun & daylight: scrub the day to see the sun's direction and height, with sunrise and sunset times
🗓️

Build the day on a strip board.

Arrange scenes into shooting days in shoot order, split a scene across days, and drag strips between days to build the plan. It's a board on Mac and iPad, and a list on iPhone — the same schedule, wherever you are.

  • Drag strips between days; split a scene across days
  • Order scenes exactly the way you'll shoot them
  • A board on Mac & iPad, a list on iPhone
On set, live
📱

Run the day,
from your pocket.

When the shoot day arrives, switch to a full-screen, check-off view of the day's scenes. Mark each shot as it's in the can, log your takes and circle the keeper — with the current setup blown up large and REF and MAP a tap away.

  • Mark shots in the can; log takes and circle the keeper
  • Live Activity on the iPhone Lock Screen & Dynamic Island
  • Step shot to shot without opening the app
  • Progress at a glance — shots done, scenes wrapped
